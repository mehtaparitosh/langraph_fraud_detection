# Case 02 — Velocity burst, step-up failed
Date: 2026-03-11
Signals: 7 rapid transactions across several gift-card and crypto merchants; new device; OTP requested.
Rule score: 55 (grey). Analyst view: possible account takeover in progress.
Action taken: step_up. Cardholder failed OTP three times; requests continued after the failures.
Outcome: DECLINED and SAR filed. Card frozen pending customer contact.
Lesson: Failed step-up on a velocity burst escalates straight to decline + SAR.
