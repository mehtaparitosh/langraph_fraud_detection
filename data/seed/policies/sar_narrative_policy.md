# SAR Narrative Policy

A Suspicious Activity Report narrative must be grounded strictly in the recorded
evidence — never speculate beyond it. Every sentence must trace to a fact in the
case (transaction fields, evidence lookups, reason codes, or the recorded action).

The narrative must cover the five W's and how:
- Who: the card/account and device involved.
- What: the transaction (amount, merchant) and the action taken.
- When: the timestamp and any velocity window.
- Where: the geo and IP context.
- Why: the reason codes and risk signals that triggered the report.
- How: the mechanism (e.g. blocklist hit, velocity burst, proxy device).

Keep it factual, concise, and free of unverified claims.
